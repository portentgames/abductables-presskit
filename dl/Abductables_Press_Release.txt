ABDUCTABLES, A FLYING SAUCER ROGUELITE IN VINTAGE COMIC-BOOK STYLE, LANDS ON STEAM DECEMBER 7, 2026

Beam up whole crowds, outrun an army that escalates to five stars, and bring a stronger saucer back every run. The free demo is on Steam now.

Portent Games, a one-person indie studio, will release Abductables on Steam for Windows PC on December 7, 2026, priced at $6.99 USD.

Abductables is an incremental roguelite set inside a 1950s sci-fi comic. You pilot a flying saucer over small-town America and pull people off the ground with your tractor beam: farmers, cows, cops and soldiers. Hold the beam over a crowd and lift as many as you can at once, because the bigger the batch, the bigger the combo when they're converted into alien-human hybrids. Every run earns credits for permanent upgrades, so each attempt sends a stronger saucer back down to Earth.

The humans hit back. The armed response climbs from police cars and searchlight helicopters to soldiers, rocket troopers, an army tank and a gunship that rakes its cannon down a lane through your ship. Radar trucks sweep away your cloak, leech drones latch onto your hull, and agents in purple sedans shut off your beam inside their rings. A rival saucer, Armaros's crew, turns up mid-shift to poach your crowds.

You fight back with Field Kits: an EMP Pulse that stalls cars and knocks aircraft out of the sky, the Arc Coil, a Decoy Saucer, a Forget-Me Flash, Chaff and a cloak. Upgrade the biomass tractor beam far enough (Heavy Lift) and you can haul the wrecks you just shot down, even a whole tank, aboard for extra points. Survive long enough and the shadowy Grigori Group sends a memo with three Directives to choose from, each adding a new rule to the rest of the run.

Everything is drawn in vintage comic print: ink outlines, halftone dots and lettered sound effects like MAYDAY! and KA-BLAM! The full game has three towns: a summer-Americana suburb, a 1950s pre-code horror town, and a desert town of cactus fields and a cemetery in 1980s horror-comic colors.
